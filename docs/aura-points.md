# Aura Points — BULK Exchange Season 1 Mechanics

Aura points (AURA) are BULK Exchange's Season 1 scoring system for pre-TGE community participation. Season 1 launched June 1, 2026.

> **Sources:** Technical architecture facts are from [docs.bulk.trade](https://docs.bulk.trade). Season 1 mechanics (AURA distribution, referrals, deposits) were announced via official BULK Exchange communications on June 1, 2026. The docs.bulk.trade/points and /referral pages are marked "coming soon" as of June 2, 2026.

---

## What Are Aura Points?

AURA are scoring units that track pre-TGE community participation. At TGE, AURA determines each participant's share of the community token allocation.

The confirmed community allocation is **30% of total BULK token supply**, per official BULK Exchange announcement (June 1, 2026). Total token supply and exact AURA-to-token conversion formula have not been published.

---

## How AURA Is Distributed

The following parameters are from the official Season 1 launch announcement:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Total weekly AURA | 1,000,000 |
| Distribution schedule | Every Saturday starting June 6, 2026 |
| Formula | USDC deposited × time held (pro-rata share) |
| Minimum deposit | $10 USDC |
| Maximum deposit | $5,000,000 USDC per account |
| Retroactive allocation | 500,000 AURA (one-time, snapshot May 31, 2026) |
| Withdrawal | Anytime, no lockup |

## How to Earn AURA

### Pre-Deposit (Primary Method)

Deposit USDC at [early.bulk.trade/deposit](https://builtonbulk.xyz/go/bulk-app). AURA accumulates proportionally to your share of total USDC held × time held across all participants. Every Saturday snapshot captures the current state.

Withdrawing and re-depositing resets your time-held clock on those funds. Uninterrupted holding maximises AURA earned.

### Referrals

Earn AURA when referred accounts hold $100 or more through the weekly snapshot. The rate is 1 AURA per $100 referred and held. No cap on referrals. Referral codes are available to all depositors from the Pre-Deposit Dashboard.

### BulkSOL Holding and Staking

BulkSOL holders and stakers receive a portion of weekly AURA. The exact split between the depositor, referral, and BulkSOL buckets has not been published. The official announcement states depositors receive the "majority" of weekly AURA.

### Retroactive Categories (Closed — June 1, 2026 Snapshot)

The following activity was eligible for the 500,000 AURA retroactive allocation:

- OG and Contributor Discord role holders
- Alphanet and paper trading competition participants
- BulkSOL holders on Exponent Finance, Loopscale, and P0
- Native BulkSOL stakers

These categories are closed. No new wallets can qualify retroactively.

---

## Dilution

1,000,000 AURA is split proportionally each Saturday among all participants. As total deposits grow, each dollar earns proportionally less AURA per week. Earlier participants lock in higher ratios before the pool scales.
